# Time Bomb Evolution — Rules Reference

This document is a complete, table-ready rules reference for **Time Bomb** with an additional section for **Evolution** content.

---

## Preliminary notes
- The game is designed for 4-6 players, with different setups for each player count.

## 1) Objective

There are two teams:

- **Sherlock (Defusers)**: reveal all required **defuse wire** cards before the game ends.
- **Moriarty (Bombers)**: reveal 4 of the same colour **bomb wire** or stop Sherlock from completing the defuse objective in time.

The game is hidden-role social deduction: players bluff, accuse, and infer alliances from choices and behavior.

---

## 2) Teams and secrecy

- Shuffle and deal one **Role/Identity** card to each player.
    - If you are four players you deal an extra role card which you do not look at and leave out of the game.
    - For 4-5 players deal 3 **Sherlock** and 2 **Moriarty** role cards.
    - For 6 players deal 4 **Sherlock** and 2 **Moriarty** role cards.
- Keep your role hidden.
- You may claim any role/team during discussion, truthfully or not.
- Team sizes depend on player count.

---

## 3) Wire setup

The full deck consists of:
- **Defuse wires** (safe cards) - 6 total
- **Bomb wires - green** - 5 total
- **Bomb wires - orange** - 5 total
- **Bomb wires - pink** - 5 total
- **Bomb wires - yellow** - 5 total
- **Bomb wires - blue** - 5 total
- **Bomb wires - red** - 5 total

To prepare the game for your player count you:
- 1. Randomly select colours of bomb wires to use. One for every player in the game.
- 2. Take all 5 bomb wires of those colours and add them into a deck and shuffle them.
- 3. Discard an amount of cards from the top equal to the number of players in the game. Do not look at these cards, just discard them face down.
- 4. Add the same number of defuse wires as players into the deck and shuffle again.

Keep all unrevealed wires private.

---

## 4) Round structure

A round is a sequence of turns where players choose wires to cut/reveal.

Typical flow:

1. Every player is dealt a hand of 5 wire cards at the start of the game and one less card for each subsequent round (so 5 cards in round 1, 4 cards in round 2, etc.). Players keep their hand of wires private.
2. The players look at their hand and try to memorise what they have got.
3. Each player shuffles their hand and places it face down in front of them.
4. If this is the first round you determine your starting player. (In this game the starting player is the player who most recently visited London)
5. This player takes their turn.
6. The game proceeds for as many turns as there are players in the game.
7. The round ends. Repeat from step 1 for the next round until an instant win/loss condition is met or the final round (round 4) ends.

Discussion/bluffing is allowed at all times unless your group sets a stricter communication house rule.

---

## 5) Turn structure

A turn is when a single player chooses one wire card from some other players pile and reveals it.

1. The active player chooses one other player.
2. That player reveals their top card by flipping it face up and placing it in the center of the table.
3. The active player checks the revealed card against the win/loss conditions (see next section).
4. The turn ends and the player chosen to reveal a card takes the next turn.

---

## 6) Instant outcomes and end conditions

Check these every time a wire is revealed:

### A) Bomb revealed

- If the 4th **bomb wire** is revealed at any time: **Moriarty team wins immediately**.

### B) Defuse objective completed

- If all **defuse wires** are revealed: **Sherlock team wins immediately**.

### C) Time/round limit reached

- If neither condition above is met before round 4 ends: **Moriarty team wins**.

---

## 7) Information and table etiquette

- Revealed wires remain visible public information.
- Unrevealed wires remain hidden.
- Do not show hidden cards to other players.
- Verbal deception is part of normal play.
- No out-of-game proof methods (no “I swear on…”, no phone notes, no coded signals).

---

## 8) Ties, mistakes, and invalid reveals

Use these practical resolutions:

- If a hidden card is accidentally exposed, keep it revealed and continue.
- If a player reveals the wrong card by mistake, that reveal stands.
- If setup was incorrect and discovered early, reset and redeal.
- If setup error is discovered late, finish the round, then replay with correct setup.

---

## 9) Recommended teaching flow (quick start)

1. Deal secret roles.
2. Build and deal wire cards for your player count.
3. Start turns and reveal wires one by one.
4. Bomb = Moriarty wins instantly.
5. All required safe wires found first = Sherlock wins.
6. If time/round limit expires first = Moriarty wins.

---

## 10) Evolution Rules (additional section)

The **Evolution** module keeps the same core win conditions but adds extra complexity.

### 10.1 What changes in Evolution

- Each colour of bomb wire is associated with a special effect that triggers when revealed.
- When setting up the game the players may choose whether to randomly select card colours or to select them based on the effects they all want in play.
- The **defuse wires** now are able to defuse specific colours of bomb wires if they've been revealed. If a defuse wire is revealed and all revealed colours of bomb wires already have a defuse wire revealed the defuse wire is placed in a separate pile. They count towards the defuse objective but do not defuse a specific colours powers.
- The **defuse wires** when defusing a bomb wire means that colour bomb cannot explode from any effect at all for the rest of the game, except for the pink bomb wires special effect.

### 10.3 Card colour effects

- **Green**: When a 3rd green bomb wire is revealed the bomb explodes. This can still be defused by a **defuse wire**.
- **Orange**: At the beginning of the 4th round, count the number of revealed orange bomb wires in the middle of the table (even if defused). This round will last as many turns as the number of players minus that number.
- **Pink**: If the last wire card that was revealed was a pink bomb wire, when a pink bomb wire is revealed the bomb explodes immediately. This cannot be defused by a **defuse wire**.
- **Yellow**: When you are choosing a colour to defuse you cannot choose yellow bomb wires.
- **Blue**: When a blue bomb wire is revealed and there are defused wire colours played the player who revealed the blue bomb wire must choose one of the defused colours to reactivate. That colour is now active again and can explode. If the defused colour that is reactivated has enough revealed bomb wires to meet its explosion condition it explodes immediately and Moriarty wins.
- **Red**: When a red bomb wire is revealed the next player must cut the wire of a randomly determined player. In the board game this is done by shuffling number cards in a pile based on the number of players and revealing one to determine the player. In a digital version you can use a random number generator or an app that can do this for you. If the current reveal was the end of a round then the round end is resolved first and then the red bomb wire effect is applied.
