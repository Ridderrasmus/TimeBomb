# Time Bomb Rules (Shorthand)
*Standard variant*

## Goal
- **Sherlock team:** reveal as many Defuse wires as players before the game ends.
- **Moriarty team:** trigger a Bomb or stall until round/turn limits run out.

## Round Flow
- Round start: players review hand and mark ready.
- Active play: current player cuts one wire from a valid target.

## Turn Constraints
- You cannot cut your own wires.

## Win Conditions
- Sherlock wins when defuse objective is completed.
- Moriarty wins if a bomb explodes (4 of the same colour bomb is revealed).
- Moriarty also wins if round 4 is finished and Sherlock hasn't won yet.
